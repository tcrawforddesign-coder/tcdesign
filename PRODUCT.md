# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Hiring managers and creative leads** at agencies and in-house marketing teams, evaluating Travis for a design role. They skim fast, judge craft from the first few images, and want to see range, process and real-world results.
- **Prospective freelance clients**, mostly small businesses and brands who need a logo, a brand identity or campaign creative. They want to know what he makes, whether it fits their brand, and how to start.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Travis Crawford, a visual designer and creative strategist based in San Antonio, Texas. It shows his work and gets the right people to contact him.

Success is a visitor who downloads the resume or reaches out (email, LinkedIn). The "Start a project" email and the logo brief are the routes for freelance work.

## Positioning

Strategy, not just visuals: Travis frames the problem and the idea behind a brand or campaign, then carries it through identity, ads, social and print. The case studies should show the thinking (challenge, approach, decisions), not only the finished images.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, Dribbble, or a direct link, often on a phone.
- Most read a home page and one or two case studies, then decide.
- Travis works full-time as a creative marketing specialist at 3Sixty Integrated (security integration, acquired by CBX Solutions). That in-house work appears as a case study.
- Freelance logo work starts from the logo brief (`/logo-questionnaire`), which emails a PDF of the answers via a Vercel function (`api/`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offers: brand identity, logos, campaigns and paid social, posters, motion pieces and visual experiments. Photography is planned (page marked "Coming soon").
- **Does not offer websites or web design.** Never list web design as a service or describe Travis as a web designer.
- Site sections: Featured work (case studies), Branding, Logos, Posters, Experiments, Photography (coming soon), About, Logo brief.
- Built with React and Vite, deployed on Vercel from the `main` branch of `tcrawforddesign-coder/tcdesign`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Travis Crawford** (not TC Studio, which is retired).
- Mark: a halftone dot monogram (a T inside an open C, made of blue dots, with a red point). Used in the header, footer, favicon and the opening intro.
- Voice: plain, direct, first person. Short sentences, no jargon.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case studies with real assets in `public/images/`: Civil Goat Coffee, Barbican Centre (concept), Yellow Bike (UX concept, team of 4), Atlas Coffee Club (paid social ads), 3Sixty Integrated (in-house marketing), Ritual Coffee, Data Dog (concept), Aluma (hidden).
- Poster archive (28 posters), experiments archive, logo set.
- Resume: `public/TravisCrawford_Resume.pdf`. Headshot: `public/brand/travis-headshot.*`.
- **No testimonials, client logos, metrics or results data exist.** Do not invent them. Concept and student projects must stay labeled as concepts.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with the work.** The first thing anyone should judge is the work itself.
2. **Show the thinking.** Every case study explains the problem and the strategy behind the visuals.
3. **Make reaching out effortless.** The resume and contact options are always one step away.
4. **Be truthful.** No invented results, clients or claims. Concept work is labeled as concept work.
5. **Speak to both audiences without splitting the site in two.**

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation and visible focus throughout, respect for reduced-motion settings (the intro and animations already do), and readable text contrast over images and the dot field.
